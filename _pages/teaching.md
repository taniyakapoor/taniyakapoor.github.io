## Teaching & Supervision

### Wageningen University & Research

At Wageningen University & Research (WUR), I am actively involved in teaching, course coordination, student supervision, and curriculum development in artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, and data science.

#### Course Coordination and Teaching
I serve as course coordinator for *Deep Learning* and contribute as a lecturer to both *Machine Learning* and *Deep Learning*. My teaching combines fundamental machine learning concepts with modern AI methods and their applications to scientific and interdisciplinary problems.

#### PhD and MSc Supervision
I co-supervise two PhD researchers working at the intersection of scientific machine learning and AI for complex systems:

- [Eva van Tegelen](https://research.wur.nl/en/persons/eva-van-tegelen/) works on model-based machine learning methodologies for identifying and quantifying resilience and tipping points in complex systems, including neural differential equation and operator-based approaches.
- [Thijs van Weezel](https://research.wur.nl/en/persons/thijs-van-weezel/) works on foundation models for agricultural sciences, with a focus on developing generalizable AI methods for scientific and agricultural applications.

I also co-supervise five master’s thesis students developing challenge-driven AI models in collaboration with research groups in greenhouse systems, meteorology, and food science. 


#### Student Projects and Curriculum Development
I have supervised student projects for *Data Science for Global Challenges* and developed teaching material for *Introduction to AI for 3D Circularity*. These activities reflect my interest in connecting AI methodology with real-world scientific and societal challenges.

#### University Teaching Qualification (UTQ)
I am currently completing the University Teaching Qualification (UTQ) programme at WUR and have obtained three partial certificates in:

- Teaching
- Supervising
- Designing


### Delft University of Technology

Previously at TU Delft, my teaching activities focused on integrating artificial intelligence and physics-informed machine learning into engineering education. I prepared tutorials and examinations for *A1*, *MUDE*, *Dynamica*, and *Data Science and AI for Engineers*, and served as an examiner for *Data Science and AI for Engineers*.

I also developed and supervised the master’s course project *Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Simulating the Dynamics of Beam Systems*, supervising nine students, and co-supervised a master’s thesis on *Neural Networks Infused with Physics for Beam Systems*.
