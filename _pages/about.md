I am an Assistant Professor in the [**Artificial Intelligence Group at WUR**](https://www.wur.nl/en/chair-groups/artificial-intelligence-group/people) and a member of [**ELLIS**](https://ellis.eu/person/taniya-kapoor) and [**4TU+.AMI**](https://www.4tu.nl/ami/Research/sri-bridgingNAML/). My research focuses on two critical challenges in AI for science: Limited data and computational cost.

My expertise is in [**Scientic machine learning and physics-informed machine learning**](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=aa6HC8MAAAAJ&hl=en), with a focus on developing generalizable, data-efficient, and computationally efficient methods for sustainable engineering. 

Prior to joining WUR as an assistant professor, I was awarded the [**Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellowship at the University of Oxford**](https://www.ox.ac.uk/news/2025-05-02-oxford-welcomes-new-schmidt-ai-science-fellows). I obtained my PhD (Sep (2021–2024)) from [**TU Delft**](https://www.tudelft.nl/citg/over-faculteit/afdelingen/engineering-structures). My thesis examiners were [**Kees Vuik**](https://diamhomes.ewi.tudelft.nl/~kvuik/Welcome_nl.html), [**Stephen Roberts**](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~sjrob/), [**Siddhartha Mishra**](https://camlab.ethz.ch/the-group/group-head.html), and [**Daniel Tartakovsky**](https://profiles.stanford.edu/daniel-tartakovsky). I also did postdoc (Oct(2024-2025)) at TU Delft for developing AI tool for complex systems. 

I completed dual master’s degrees (fully-funded) in high-performance scientific computing at [**Université de Lille**](https://hpc.univ-lille.fr/en/) and in mathematics at [**South Asian University**](https://sau.int/department-of-mathematics/). I also completed a research internship at [**ETH Zürich (CAMLab)**](https://camlab.ethz.ch/the-group/group-head.html). I obtained my bachelor’s degree in mathematics from the [**University of Delhi**](https://maths.du.ac.in/).
